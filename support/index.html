Contact support

Where are my recordings?

Each session is a folder of CSV files plus session.json in the app's Documents folder. Open the Files app › On My iPhone › SensorForge, or connect the phone to a Mac and use Finder. You can also share a session from the Logs tab.

My sensor doesn't connect

Make sure Bluetooth is on, the sensor is awake and worn (chest straps need moisture), and it isn't connected to another app such as Polar Flow. Then remove it in SensorForge and add it again.

Weather or location is missing from a session

Location is off by default. Turn it on in Settings › Environment and allow location access when iOS asks. If a fix isn't available within 10 seconds, the reason is written to the notes field of the session file.

Analysing your recordings

Free example notebooks for Python (Jupyter, VS Code or Google Colab) load a session folder and show quality checks, plots of every signal, PPG spectrograms and heart-rate variability: github.com/Nsantis/sensorforge-examples. Copy your session folder into the repository's data/ folder and set LOG at the top of a notebook. Questions about the notebooks can be asked as issues in that repository.

Restoring SensorForge Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. On a new or reset device, open the paywall and tap Restore purchases.
